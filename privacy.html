Privacy Policy

Self Quest: Small Doses — Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

Self Quest: Small Doses is a micro-wellness experience designed for relaxation and focus. Your privacy matters to us, and we want you to feel safe using our app. This policy explains what data we do and do not collect.

Data We Collect

None. Self Quest does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no login, no forms, no cookies, and no analytics tracking within the app. Your experience is completely private.

Third-Party Services

Self Quest does not use any third-party services that collect user data. The app runs entirely in your browser or as an installed application on your device. No data is sent to external servers.

Children's Privacy

Self Quest is designed to be safe and appropriate for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no risk of children's data being gathered or shared.

Music and Content

All music, visuals, and affirmations within Self Quest are original works created by Jeseka Price and are the property of Price Royalty Publishing (BMI). All rights reserved.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Since we do not collect user data, we have no way to notify users directly of changes.
